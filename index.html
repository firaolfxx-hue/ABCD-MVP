About ABCD School

Our story, our mission, and the values that guide everything we do.

Our Story

Fifteen years of shaping Ethiopia's next generation

ABCD School was founded in 2010 with a simple, ambitious vision — to create a school where academic excellence and genuine care go hand in hand.

What started as a small primary school has grown into a full Kindergarten through Grade 12 institution serving over 1,200 students. But our core promise hasn't changed: every child is known, challenged, and supported.

Today, our graduates are studying at top universities in Ethiopia and abroad — but more importantly, they leave us as thoughtful, capable, and kind young people.

Milestones

Our journey so far

2010

The beginning

ABCD School opens its doors with Kindergarten through Grade 4, welcoming its first 80 students.

2014

Primary complete

The school expands to Grade 8, becoming a full primary institution with modern facilities.

2018

Secondary campus

Grades 9 and 10 are added, along with new science labs and a dedicated library.

2021

Full K-12 school

ABCD becomes a complete Kindergarten through Grade 12 institution, serving over 900 students.

2025

Today

Over 1,200 students, 45 expert teachers, and a growing community of proud alumni worldwide.
